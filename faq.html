Costs vary depending on your individual circumstances and the services required, but many families should plan for an overall budget in the range of US$50,000–70,000. During your initial strategy conversation, we'll help you understand the likely costs for your particular situation and where those costs come from.

A typical journey can take around 12–18 months from getting started through birth, although every situation is different. Timing can be affected by factors including matching, IVF treatment and pregnancy, so we'll help you build a realistic timeline based on your circumstances.

People pursue this path in Colombia through many different family structures, including single intended parents and married or unmarried couples, both LGBTQ+ and heterosexual. Because every family's legal circumstances are different, eligibility should be confirmed with a qualified Colombian attorney before proceeding.

Most intended parents should plan to spend approximately 3–8 weeks in Colombia around the birth and post-birth process. The exact time depends in large part on your nationality and the legal and travel documentation required for your child before you can return home.
