Costs and Timing

Most intended parents should plan for an overall budget in the region of US$50,000–70,000, although individual circumstances can change this considerably.

Medical treatment, legal work, travel, documentation and other variables all affect the final amount. During your strategy consultation, we'll help you understand what a realistic budget looks like for your situation rather than relying on a single headline number.

A reasonable expectation for the overall journey is approximately 12–18 months, but there is no guaranteed timeline.

Creating embryos, finding the right match, medical treatment, pregnancy and post-birth documentation all introduce variables. Some people move quickly through the process, while others need considerably longer.

CunaDream's coordination fee is paid in stages rather than all at once. Your payment schedule will be set out clearly in your agreement before we begin working together, so you'll know what is due and when.

Payments to medical, legal and other professionals are separate and follow their own schedules. We'll help you keep track of those payments throughout the process.
