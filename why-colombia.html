WHY COLOMBIA?

A more accessible path, without losing the human side.

Colombia combines sophisticated private medical care with significantly more accessible costs than many international alternatives. But what makes the experience distinctive goes beyond affordability: it’s also the warmth, personal relationships and human connection that surround the process.

01 — A more accessible overall path

International family building can become extraordinarily expensive. Colombia offers access to experienced professionals and sophisticated private medical care at an overall cost that can be substantially lower than comparable paths in the United States and Canada.

CunaDream also works differently from a traditional international agency. Rather than placing the entire journey inside one large agency package, we help you build a team of trusted independent professionals and coordinate the different parts around you.

02 — Medical care you can feel confident in

Bogotá has a sophisticated healthcare sector, with modern fertility centers, specialist physicians and internationally recognized hospitals. For intended parents accustomed to healthcare in Europe or North America, the standard of care feels reassuringly familiar.

But quality and experience vary between providers. Knowing which professionals to work with matters. CunaDream draws on firsthand experience of Bogotá’s medical landscape to connect you with professionals we know and trust.

03 — An inclusive path for different families

Colombia recognizes families in many forms. Single intended parents, married and unmarried couples, and LGBTQ+ and heterosexual families may have options here that aren’t available to them in other countries.

Every family’s circumstances are different, particularly when an international process crosses more than one legal system. That’s why experienced Colombian legal counsel is an essential part of the team CunaDream helps you put in place.

04 — A process built around people

The relationship with your gestational carrier matters. We encourage intended parents to communicate regularly, spend time getting to know her and meet in person during the process.

Matching and day-to-day support are handled by a local coordinator, while medical and psychological screening is carried out by the appropriate professionals. CunaDream stays connected across the process and can help with communication and interpretation when needed.

For us, this should never feel like a purely transactional relationship.

05 — Local knowledge makes a difference

Working across countries and cultures isn’t always predictable. Timelines shift, information can change quickly and sometimes getting an answer requires knowing when — and how — to follow up.

CunaDream was built from firsthand experience of dealing with Colombia. We understand how things work locally, know the professionals involved and help keep things moving when the process doesn’t follow a perfectly straight line.

06 — A culture that feels personal

One of the things that stayed with us most from our own experience in Colombia was the warmth of the people. Family and children are deeply valued, and the attention and affection shown to children is often remarkable.

That warmth carries into the relationships you build along the way. In a process that can otherwise feel highly medical, legal and administrative, the human side matters.
