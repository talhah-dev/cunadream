OUR STORY

Cuna means “cradle” in Spanish — a nod to Colombia and to the reason CunaDream exists.

Like many intended parents, I explored adoption and surrogacy across several countries. Yet the complexity and high costs made having a baby seem almost impossible for us. So I began researching, eventually bringing together a group of professionals in Colombia who helped us build our family.

Going through the process ourselves taught us what it takes to fit all the disparate pieces together. I created CunaDream so other intended parents have an easier way forward, with the coordination and trusted connections I wish we'd had when we started.

Costs vary depending on your individual circumstances and the services required, but many families should plan for an overall budget in the range of US$50,000–70,000. A strategy consultation can help you understand the likely costs and considerations for your particular situation.

A typical journey can take around 12–18 months from getting started through birth, although every situation is different. Timing can be affected by factors including matching, IVF treatment and pregnancy, so we'll help you build a realistic timeline based on your circumstances.

Families of all kinds pursue this path in Colombia. Whether you're single or a couple, married or unmarried, straight or LGBTQ+, Colombia offers an inclusive path to parenthood.

Most intended parents should plan to spend approximately 3–8 weeks in Colombia around the birth and post-birth process. The exact time depends in large part on your nationality and the legal and travel documentation required for your child before you can return home.
