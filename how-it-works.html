HOW IT WORKS

A coordinated path from first decisions to the journey home

Building a family in Colombia involves medical, legal and local professionals working on different parts of the process. CunaDream helps keep those pieces connected, so you understand what is happening, what comes next and who is responsible for what.

01

Strategy & Preparation

Before anything begins, we look at your situation, priorities and expectations and build a realistic plan for moving forward.

  • In-depth strategy consultation based on your individual circumstances and goals

  • Assess whether Colombia is an appropriate path for you

  • Map out likely costs, payment stages and a realistic timeline

  • Discuss embryo status, genetic material and what travel to Colombia is likely to involve

  • Identify the decisions and preparations that need to happen first

  • Develop a practical plan for moving forward

02

Build Your Team & Begin Matching

Once you have a plan, we bring together the professionals you'll need in Colombia and help get the different parts of your journey moving.

  • Introduce you to the local coordinator responsible for gestational-carrier matching and support

  • Help you choose the medical and legal professionals appropriate for your circumstances

  • Begin the matching process with the local coordinator

  • Coordinate communication between the legal, medical and local teams

  • Make sure you understand who is responsible for each part of the process

  • Remain your central point of coordination when questions cross between professionals

03

Treatment, Pregnancy & Ongoing Coordination

Once treatment is underway, your attorney, medical team and local coordinator remain responsible for their respective areas. CunaDream helps keep you connected throughout the months ahead.

  • Coordinate communication between you and your Colombian team

  • Track important milestones, appointments, payments, documents and decisions

  • Anticipate upcoming steps and follow up when something needs attention

  • Clarify information when language or cultural differences arise

  • Help you understand what you're being asked to do and why

  • Step in when something needs to be coordinated across multiple professionals

  • Help you maintain regular communication with your gestational carrier, including interpretation during calls when useful

  • Help you plan your pre-birth time in Colombia and meeting your gestational carrier in person

04

Legal & Administrative Coordination

Your attorney provides the legal advice. CunaDream helps keep the related administrative pieces organized and connected to the rest of the process.

  • Coordinate with your Colombian attorney throughout the legal process

  • Help you understand which documents and information are required

  • Keep track of administrative steps and deadlines

  • Support and coordinate document collection, translation, notarization and apostille requirements where appropriate

  • Make sure legal questions are directed to the attorney responsible for advising you

05

Birth & Time in Colombia

As the birth approaches, the focus shifts from planning at a distance to coordinating what needs to happen on the ground.

  • Help coordinate practical arrangements surrounding the birth

  • Help you understand what to expect and who is responsible for what

  • Coordinate with the local team as plans evolve

  • Help resolve logistical issues that arise during your stay

  • Remain available as a central point of coordination during this critical period

06

Documentation & Going Home

After the birth, legal, administrative and practical processes need to move forward at the same time. Your attorneys and consular authorities handle their respective areas; CunaDream helps keep the overall process moving.

  • Coordinate communication between you, your attorney and the local team

  • Help you understand what needs to happen, in what order and who is responsible

  • Track key documents, appointments and deadlines

  • Follow up when information or documentation is outstanding

  • Help coordinate the practical arrangements surrounding your stay and eventual departure

  • Stay involved until the required process in Colombia is complete and you're ready to travel home

WHO DOES WHAT?

One journey. Different areas of expertise.

CunaDream

Overall coordination, timelines, communication, interpretation, practical guidance and keeping the different parts connected.

Medical team

IVF, screening, treatment and medical care.

Colombian attorney

Legal advice, agreements and Colombian legal procedures.

Local coordinator

Gestational-carrier matching and day-to-day local support.

Your home-country professionals

Any legal, consular or administrative requirements that apply when bringing your child home.
