How It Works

Navigating international surrogacy involves many moving pieces. Here is a clear look at how we guide you through each stage of the process in Colombia, bringing all the professionals together into one connected journey.

01

Strategy & Preparation

Understand your situation, assess whether Colombia is appropriate, and build a realistic roadmap.

02

Build Your Colombian Team

Connect you with the appropriate legal, medical and local professionals and establish how everyone will work together.

03

Ongoing Coordination

Stay on top of communication, milestones, decisions, documents and the many moving pieces involved.

04

Legal & Administrative

Coordinate with your attorney and other specialists so you know what is needed, when it is needed, and who is responsible.

05

Birth & Local Support

Coordinate the practical details surrounding the birth and provide a central point of contact while you are in Colombia.

06

Documentation & Going Home

Coordinate with the professionals responsible for post-birth legal documentation, citizenship, passports and travel, and help you navigate the transition home.

01

Strategy & Preparation

  • In-depth strategy call based on your individual situation, goals and expectations

  • Assess whether Colombia is an appropriate path for you

  • Explain the overall process, key decisions, likely costs and timelines

  • Identify what needs to be in place before you begin

  • Develop a realistic roadmap for moving forward

02

Building the Right Team

  • Connect you with the gestational carrier coordinator

  • Coordinate the relationships between the legal, medical and local teams

  • Help ensure everyone understands their role and responsibilities

  • Remain the central point of contact when questions cross between different professionals

03

Ongoing Coordination

  • Coordinate communication between future parent(s) and the Colombian team

  • Track important milestones, appointments, documents and decisions

  • Help anticipate what needs to happen next

  • Translate/clarify information when language or cultural differences arise

  • Help future parent(s) understand what they're being asked to do and why

  • Step in when something needs to be coordinated across multiple parties

04

Legal and Administrative Coordination

  • Coordinate with the Colombian attorney on the legal process

  • Help future parent(s) understand what documents and information are required

  • Keep track of administrative steps and deadlines

  • Coordinate document collection, translation, notarization/apostille requirements, etc., where appropriate

  • Ensure questions are directed to the attorney when legal advice is required

05

Birth and Time in Colombia

  • Help coordinate arrangements around the birth

  • Help future parent(s) understand what to expect and who is responsible for what

  • Coordinate with the local team as plans change

  • Help resolve logistical issues that arise during their stay

  • Remain available as a central point of coordination during this critical period

06

Post-Birth Coordination

  • Coordinate communication between you, your attorney and the local team following the birth

  • Help you understand the sequence of post-birth legal and administrative steps and what is required from you

  • Keep track of key documents, appointments and deadlines across the different professionals involved

  • Coordinate practical arrangements around your departure from Colombia while your legal and consular professionals handle the required documentation
